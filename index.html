Simplifying Rural Complaint Resolution

Rural Resolve helps rural citizens easily raise complaints, monitor progress, and receive timely solutions with full transparency.

Features of Rural Resolve

🎤

Voice Support

Citizens who cannot read or write can submit complaints using voice commands. This ensures accessibility for everyone.

🌐

Language Accessibility

Supports both English and Tamil, allowing rural citizens to submit complaints in their preferred language.

📊

Status Tracking

Track complaint progress in real time and receive email updates for complete transparency.
