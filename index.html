Simplifying Rural Complaint Resolution

Rural Resolve helps rural citizens easily raise complaints, monitor progress, and receive timely solutions with full transparency.

Features of Rural Resolve

🎤

Voice Support

Citizens who cannot read or write can submit complaints using voice commands. This feature ensures equal access for everyone.

🌐

Language Accessibility

Supports both English and Tamil so rural citizens can easily understand and submit complaints.

📊

Status Tracking & Email Updates

Track complaint progress in real time and receive email updates.

Why Choose Rural Resolve?

🏛️

Direct Access to Higher Authorities

Complaints are forwarded beyond local offices to ensure accountability and real solutions.

🎙️

Voice & Multi-Language Support

Citizens can register complaints using voice input with support for Tamil and English.

📊

Transparent Tracking & Analytics

Area-wise analytics identify affected regions while email notifications keep citizens updated.
